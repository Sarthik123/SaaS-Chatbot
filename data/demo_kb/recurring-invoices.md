# Recurring invoices

## What a recurring invoice is
A recurring invoice is a template that creates a new invoice for you on a schedule. It is ideal for subscriptions, retainers and rent.

## Set one up
Open any existing invoice and choose More > Make recurring, or go to Invoices > Recurring > New. Pick how often it repeats: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Then choose a start date and, if you want, an end date or a total number of invoices.

## Send automatically or review first
Choose "Send automatically" to email each invoice to your customer on the day it is created. Choose "Save as draft" if you want to check each one before it goes out. Recurring invoices are created at 06:00 UTC on the scheduled date.

## How many schedules you can have
The number of active recurring schedules depends on your plan. Starter allows up to 3, Growth allows up to 25, and Business has no limit.

## Pause, resume or end a schedule
Go to Invoices > Recurring, open a schedule and choose Pause, Resume or End. Pausing never deletes invoices that were already created. Dates skipped while a schedule was paused are not back-filled when you resume.

## Changing the price
Edit the recurring template to change the amount or the line items. The change applies from the next invoice only. Invoices that were already created stay as they are.

## Reminders and late fees
Invoices created from a schedule follow your normal reminder and late-fee settings. See "Automatic payment reminders" and "Late fees".
