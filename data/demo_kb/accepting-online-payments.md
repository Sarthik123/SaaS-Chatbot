# Accepting online payments

## Connect a payment provider
Acme Invoicing works with a payment provider called PayLink. To connect it, go to Settings > Payments > Connect PayLink. Only the Owner and Admins can do this. Setup takes about 10 minutes because PayLink needs to verify your business.

## How your customers pay
When PayLink is connected, every invoice email and invoice link has a Pay now button. Your customer can pay by credit card or by bank transfer.

## Fees
Acme Invoicing does not add any fee of its own. PayLink charges 2.9% plus $0.30 for each card payment, and 0.8% for each bank transfer, capped at $5.

## Payouts
PayLink sends your money to your bank account on every business day. Payouts usually arrive within 2 business days.

## Paid status updates automatically
When a payment succeeds, the invoice is marked as paid, reminders stop, and your customer receives a receipt by email.

## Payments received outside Acme Invoicing
If a customer pays you in cash, by cheque or by a bank transfer straight into your account, open the invoice and choose Record payment. Enter the amount and the date.

## Refunds
You can refund a Pay now payment from the invoice. See "Refunds and credit notes".
