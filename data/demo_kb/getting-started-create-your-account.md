# Getting started: create your Acme Invoicing account

## Sign up
Go to the Acme Invoicing sign-up page and enter your name, your work email address and a password of at least 12 characters. We send a verification email straight away. Click the link inside it within 24 hours to activate your account. If the link expires, return to the sign-in page and choose "Resend verification email".

## Your 14-day free trial
Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. You do not need a credit card to start. When the trial ends, your account becomes read-only until you choose a plan on the Settings > Billing page. Nothing is deleted when the trial ends, so you can pick a plan at any time and carry on where you left off.

## Set up your company profile
After you verify your email, a setup checklist asks for four things: your company name, your business address, your default currency and, optionally, your logo. You can change all of these later under Settings > Company. The business address and company name appear in the header of every invoice you send.

## Send your first invoice
The last step of the checklist walks you through creating your first invoice. If you would rather do it later, open Invoices in the left menu and click New invoice. The article "Create an invoice" explains each field.

## Invite your team
On the Growth plan you can invite up to 5 users during the trial. See "Team roles and permissions" for what each role can do.

## Need help?
Our team is available Monday to Friday. See "Support hours and how to contact us" for the details.
