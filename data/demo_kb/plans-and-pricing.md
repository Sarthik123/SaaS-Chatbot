# Plans and pricing

## The three plans
Acme Invoicing has three plans. Prices are in US dollars per month when billed monthly: Starter $12, Growth $29 and Business $59.

## What is included
- Starter: 1 user, up to 25 invoices per month, up to 3 recurring schedules, one currency.
- Growth: up to 5 users, unlimited invoices, up to 25 recurring schedules, multiple currencies, accounting integrations and required 2FA for the team.
- Business: up to 15 users, unlimited invoices and recurring schedules, priority email support and the option to remove the Acme badge from your invoices.

## Annual billing
If you pay yearly you get 2 months free, because you pay for 10 months instead of 12. The yearly prices are Starter $120, Growth $290 and Business $590.

## Free trial
Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card is needed.

## Change your plan
Go to Settings > Billing > Change plan. An upgrade starts immediately and you pay only the difference for the rest of the period. A downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

## How you pay
We accept major credit and debit cards for your subscription.
