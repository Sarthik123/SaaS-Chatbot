# Reset your password

## If you forgot your password
On the sign-in page click "Forgot password?" and enter the email address you used to sign up. We email you a reset link that is valid for 60 minutes and can be used only once.

## Choose a new password
Your new password must be at least 12 characters long. You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.

## If the email does not arrive
- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Wait 5 minutes, as emails are sometimes delayed.
- Make sure you typed the same address you used to sign up.
- Request another link. Only the most recent link works.

## If your account is locked
After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes. You can wait for the lock to end, or reset your password, which unlocks the account straight away.

## Change your password while signed in
Go to Settings > Security > Change password. You will be asked for your current password first.

## If you use two-factor authentication
After a password reset you still need your authentication code to sign in. If you have lost access to your device, see "Two-factor authentication (2FA)".
