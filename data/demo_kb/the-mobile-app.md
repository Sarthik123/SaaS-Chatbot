# The Acme Invoicing mobile app

## Where to get it
The Acme Invoicing app is available for iPhone and for Android phones. Search for "Acme Invoicing" in your app store. The app is free with every plan.

## What you can do in the app
- Create and send invoices.
- Record payments.
- Add and edit customers.
- See your dashboard with unpaid and overdue invoices.

## Signing in
Use the same email address and password as on the website. If you turn it on, you can unlock the app with Face ID or your fingerprint instead of typing your password each time. If you use two-factor authentication, the app asks for your code as usual.

## Working offline
You can create invoice drafts without an internet connection. They are saved on your device and sync automatically once you are back online. Sending an invoice always needs an internet connection.

## What is only on the website
Some features are available only on the website: importing customers from CSV, exporting all your data, team and security settings, and tax setup. The mobile app shows your tax rates but cannot change them.
