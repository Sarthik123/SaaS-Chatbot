# Taxes and VAT

## Add a tax rate
Go to Settings > Taxes > Add tax rate. Enter a name such as "VAT 20%", the percentage, and optionally the region it applies to. You can then apply the rate to any line item. One line item can carry up to 2 tax rates.

## Prices with or without tax
On each invoice you can choose "Prices include tax" or "Prices exclude tax". The default is "Prices exclude tax". If you change the setting after you have added line items, the totals are recalculated straight away.

## How tax is rounded
Tax is calculated for each line and rounded to 2 decimal places. The invoice total is the sum of the rounded lines. If your totals differ by a cent from another system, this is usually the reason.

## VAT numbers
Add your own VAT number under Settings > Company. It is printed in the invoice header. You can also store each customer's VAT number on the customer record, and it will appear on their invoices.

## EU reverse charge
If your customer is in a different EU country and has a VAT number, you can tick "Reverse charge" on the invoice. All tax lines are set to 0% and the note "Reverse charge applies" is printed on the invoice. Acme Invoicing does not check VAT numbers against any official register, so please verify them yourself.

## Tax reports
Open Reports > Tax summary to see the tax you collected for each rate over any date range. You can export the report as a CSV file.
