# Refunds and credit notes

This article covers two different things: refunding your customers, and refunding your own Acme Invoicing subscription.

## Credit notes for your customers
To give a customer money back or reduce what they owe, open the invoice and choose More > Issue credit note. A credit note can be for the full amount or for part of it. Credit notes are numbered CN-0001, CN-0002 and so on.

## Apply it or refund it
A credit note can be applied to a future invoice for the same customer. If your customer paid online with the Pay now button, you can also refund the money. It goes back to the original card or bank account and usually appears within 5 to 10 business days. Payments you recorded manually, such as cash or a cheque, must be refunded outside Acme Invoicing.

## Void an invoice
A sent invoice cannot be deleted. If you issued one by mistake and nobody has paid it, open it and choose More > Void. A voided invoice keeps its number and shows a VOID stamp, so your numbering stays complete.

## Refund of your Acme Invoicing subscription
If you ask within 14 days of your first paid charge, we refund that charge in full. Email our support team to request it. Later charges are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time. See "Cancel your subscription".
