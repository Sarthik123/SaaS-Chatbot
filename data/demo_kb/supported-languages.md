# Supported languages

## App language
The Acme Invoicing app and website are available in English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. To change the language, go to Settings > Profile > Language. The setting is personal, so each team member can choose their own.

## Invoice language
The invoices and emails your customers see can be written in seven languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Dutch. Choose the language on each customer record, or change it on a single invoice. Fixed text such as "Invoice", "Due date" and "Total" is translated automatically.

## Dates and numbers
Dates and numbers follow the language you choose, for example 31/12/2026 or 12/31/2026. You can override the format under Settings > Company > Formats.

## What is not translated
Text you type yourself, such as line item descriptions, notes and the footer, stays exactly as you wrote it. Payment reminder emails use the language of the customer, but if you edited the message text, your edited text is sent as it is.
