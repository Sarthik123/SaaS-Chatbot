# Automatic payment reminders

## The default schedule
By default Acme Invoicing emails your customer three times: 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 days after the due date if the invoice is still unpaid. Reminders stop automatically as soon as the invoice is marked as paid or is voided.

## Turn reminders on or off
Go to Settings > Invoicing > Reminders. You can switch each of the three reminders on or off and change the number of days before or after the due date.

## Customize the message
You can edit the text of each reminder email. These placeholders are filled in automatically: {customer_name}, {invoice_number}, {amount_due}, {due_date} and {pay_link}. Keep {pay_link} in the message so your customer can pay with one click.

## Who receives them
Reminders are emailed to the billing contacts on the customer record. Each customer can have up to 3 email addresses.

## Pause reminders for one invoice
Open the invoice and choose More > Pause reminders. This is useful when a customer has promised to pay on a certain day. Resume them from the same menu.

## Overdue invoices and late fees
If you have switched on late fees, an overdue invoice can also receive a fee in addition to reminders. The article "Late fees" explains how fees are calculated and when they are added.
