# Create an invoice

## Start a new invoice
Click Invoices in the left menu, then click New invoice. Choose an existing customer from the list, or click Add new customer to create one on the spot.

## Add line items
For each line enter a description, a quantity and a unit price. One invoice can have up to 100 line items. If you have set up tax rates in Settings > Taxes, you can apply them line by line.

## Invoice number and due date
Invoice numbers are created automatically in the format INV-0001, INV-0002 and so on. You can change the prefix under Settings > Invoicing. The payment terms default to Net 14, which means the invoice is due 14 days after the issue date. Other choices are Net 7, Net 30 and Net 60, or you can pick any custom due date.

## Notes and attachments
Use the Note field to add a message for your customer, such as bank details or a thank-you line. You can attach up to 3 files to one invoice, and each file can be up to 5 MB.

## Save, send or download
- Save draft keeps your work so you can finish later.
- Send emails the invoice to your customer with a Pay now link, if you have connected online payments.
- Download PDF saves a copy to your computer.
- Copy link gives you a view-only link you can share yourself.

## After you send
A sent invoice cannot be deleted. If you sent one by mistake, you can void it. See "Refunds and credit notes" for how voiding works.
