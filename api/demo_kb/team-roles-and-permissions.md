# Team roles and permissions

## How many users you can have
The number of users depends on your plan: Starter includes 1 user, Growth up to 5 users, and Business up to 15 users. To add someone, go to Settings > Team > Invite user.

## The four roles
- Owner: can do everything, including billing, deleting the account and transferring ownership. There is exactly one Owner per account.
- Admin: can do everything except manage billing, delete the account or transfer ownership.
- Accountant: can create and edit invoices, record payments, run reports and export data. An Accountant cannot invite users or change settings.
- Viewer: can see invoices, customers and reports but cannot change anything.

## Change a role or remove a user
The Owner and Admins can open Settings > Team, click a person and choose a new role or Remove. Removing a user does not delete the invoices that person created.

## Invitations
An invitation email expires after 7 days. If it expires, open Settings > Team and choose Resend invitation.

## Security for your team
On the Growth and Business plans you can require two-factor authentication for everyone on the team. See "Two-factor authentication (2FA)".
