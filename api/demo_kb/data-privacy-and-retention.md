# Data privacy and retention

## What we store
We store your account details, your team members, your customers, your invoices and payments, and the logos and attachments you upload.

## How we use your data
We use your data only to run the service for you. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your customers' information for advertising.

## How long we keep it
We keep your data while your account is active. After you cancel, your account is read-only for 90 days and then permanently deleted. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 30 days after the deletion.

## Delete a customer's personal data
Open the customer under Customers and choose Delete personal data. We remove the name, email address, phone number and address, and replace them with "Deleted customer". The invoice amounts stay, because you need them for your accounting records.

## Take your data with you
You can export everything at any time. See "Export your data".
