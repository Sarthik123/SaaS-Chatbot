# Export your data

## Export a list
The Customers, Invoices and Payments pages each have an Export button. It downloads the list as a CSV file. Dates in the file use the format YYYY-MM-DD, and amounts use a dot as the decimal separator.

## Download invoice PDFs
Open an invoice and choose Download PDF. To download many at once, go to the Invoices list, tick the invoices you want and choose Download PDFs. You can select up to 50 invoices at a time.

## Export everything
To take a full copy of your account, go to Settings > Data > Export all data. We create a ZIP file that holds CSV files for customers, invoices, credit notes and payments, plus a PDF of every invoice. When it is ready, we email a download link to the account Owner. Large accounts can take up to 1 hour to prepare.

## Who can export
The Owner, Admin and Accountant roles can export data. The Viewer role cannot.

## Before you cancel
If you plan to cancel your subscription, export your data first. See "Cancel your subscription" and "Data privacy and retention" for what happens to your data afterwards.

## Web only
Exporting everything is available on the website only, not in the mobile app.
