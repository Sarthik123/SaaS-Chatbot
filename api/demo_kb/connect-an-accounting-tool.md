# Connect an accounting tool

## Supported tools
Acme Invoicing can connect to two accounting tools: Ledgerly and BookNest. Integrations are available on the Growth and Business plans.

## How to connect
1. Go to Settings > Integrations and choose Ledgerly or BookNest.
2. Click Connect.
3. Sign in to your accounting tool and approve access.
Only the Owner and Admins can connect or disconnect an integration.

## What is synced
Invoices, credit notes and payments are sent from Acme Invoicing to your accounting tool every 30 minutes. The sync works in one direction only: changes you make in the accounting tool are not copied back to Acme Invoicing.

## Map your accounts
The first time you connect, you match each of your tax rates and your sales income to an account in the accounting tool. You can change the matching later under Settings > Integrations.

## Disconnect
Choose Settings > Integrations > Disconnect. Records that were already synced stay in your accounting tool.

## If the sync stops
The error ERR-5003 means the access token has expired. Click Reconnect on the Integrations page and approve access again. More fixes are in "Troubleshooting common problems".
