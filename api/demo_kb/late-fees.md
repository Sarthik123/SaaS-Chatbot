# Late fees

## How late fees work
A late fee is an extra charge added to an invoice that is past its due date. Late fees are switched off by default. Turn them on under Settings > Invoicing > Late fees.

## Fee types
You can choose between two types of fee.
- Flat amount: a fixed amount, for example $10, added for each overdue month.
- Percentage: a share of the invoice total. The default is 1.5% per month.

## Grace period
The grace period is the number of days after the due date before the first fee is added. The default is 3 days. You can set it anywhere from 0 to 30 days.

## The cap
The total of all late fees on one invoice is capped at 25% of the original invoice total. You can set a lower cap, but you cannot raise it above 25%.

## When fees are added
After the grace period, one fee is added for each overdue month. Fees are added at 06:00 UTC and appear as a separate line on the invoice. Your customer is told about the new amount in the next reminder email.

## A word of caution
Rules about late fees differ between countries and between types of contract. Check the rules that apply to you before you switch late fees on. Acme Invoicing cannot give legal advice.
