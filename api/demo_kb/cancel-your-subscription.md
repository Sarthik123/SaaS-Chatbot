# Cancel your subscription

## Who can cancel
Only the account Owner can cancel the subscription. Admins and other roles do not see the Cancel button.

## How to cancel
Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription and confirm. You will see the date your access ends.

## What happens after you cancel
You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that your account becomes read-only for 90 days. During those 90 days you can still sign in, look at your invoices and export your data, but you cannot create or send anything. After 90 days the account and all its data are permanently deleted.

## Changed your mind?
Within those 90 days, go to Settings > Billing > Reactivate. Choose a plan and everything is back exactly as you left it.

## Recurring invoices
When your subscription ends, your recurring schedules stop. No new invoices are created while the account is read-only.

## Refunds
If you cancel within 14 days of your first paid charge and ask us, you get a full refund. See "Refunds and credit notes". Charges after that are not refunded.

## Before you go
Export your data first. See "Export your data". The article "Data privacy and retention" explains how long we keep information.
