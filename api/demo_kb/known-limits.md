# Known limits

## Numbers and sizes
- Line items on one invoice: up to 100.
- Attachments on one invoice: up to 3 files, each up to 5 MB.
- Customers in one account: up to 10,000.
- CSV import: up to 5,000 rows and 5 MB per file.
- Download PDFs in bulk: up to 50 invoices at a time.
- Logo file: up to 2 MB.
- Invoice footer text: up to 300 characters.
- Email addresses per customer for reminders: up to 3.
- Tax rates on one line item: up to 2.

## Limits that depend on your plan
Users, invoices per month and recurring schedules differ by plan. See "Plans and pricing".

## Things you cannot do
- You cannot delete a sent invoice. You can void it instead.
- You cannot create custom roles. There are four fixed roles: Owner, Admin, Accountant and Viewer.
- You cannot change the invoice number after the invoice has been sent.
- You cannot change your default currency after you have sent your first invoice, without contacting support.
- The accounting integration works in one direction only, from Acme Invoicing to your accounting tool.

## Features only on the website
CSV import, exporting all data, team and security settings and tax setup are not available in the mobile app.
