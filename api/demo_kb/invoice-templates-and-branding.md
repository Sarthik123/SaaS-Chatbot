# Invoice templates and branding

## Add your logo
Go to Settings > Branding and upload your logo. PNG, JPG and SVG files are accepted, up to 2 MB. The logo appears at the top of every invoice and in the Pay now page.

## Brand colour
Enter your brand colour as a hex code, for example #1A73E8. It is used for headings, buttons and lines on your invoices.

## Choose a template
Acme Invoicing has three invoice templates: Classic, Modern and Compact. The template you choose applies to all your invoices, including ones you sent earlier when someone opens them again. A live preview shows how your invoice will look before you save.

## Footer text
You can add a short message at the bottom of every invoice, such as your bank details or "Thank you for your business". The footer can be up to 300 characters long.

## The "Sent with Acme Invoicing" badge
Invoices on the Starter and Growth plans show a small "Sent with Acme Invoicing" badge in the footer. On the Business plan you can remove it in Settings > Branding.

## Invoice number prefix
The prefix in front of invoice numbers, such as INV-, is set under Settings > Invoicing. See "Create an invoice".

## Language
Invoices can be written in several languages. See "Supported languages".
