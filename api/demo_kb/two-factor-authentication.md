# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

## What it is
Two-factor authentication adds a second step to signing in. After your password, you enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot sign in without the code.

## Turn it on
1. Go to Settings > Security > Two-factor authentication and click Turn on.
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
3. Enter the first 6-digit code the app shows to confirm.

## Supported methods
Acme Invoicing supports authenticator apps that create time-based 6-digit codes.

## Recovery codes
When you turn on 2FA we show you 10 one-time recovery codes. Save them somewhere safe, such as a password manager. Each code works only once, and you will not be shown them again.

## If you lose your device
Sign in with one of your recovery codes, then set up 2FA again on your new device. If you have no recovery codes left, email our support team from the address on your account. We verify your identity first, which can take up to 2 business days.

## Require 2FA for your team
On the Growth and Business plans, open Settings > Security and switch on "Require 2FA for all users". People who have not set it up are asked to do so the next time they sign in.
