# Multiple currencies

## Which plans include it
Invoicing in more than one currency is available on the Growth and Business plans. On the Starter plan, all invoices use your account's default currency.

## Supported currencies
Acme Invoicing supports 40 currencies, including USD, EUR, GBP, INR, AUD, CAD and JPY. The full list appears in the currency drop-down on any invoice.

## Invoice in your customer's currency
You can set a default currency on each customer record, or choose a currency on each invoice. The customer sees the amount, and the Pay now page, in that currency.

## Exchange rates
Exchange rates are refreshed once a day at 06:00 UTC. The rate is locked at the moment you send the invoice, so the value in your own currency does not change afterwards, even if the market rate moves.

## Reports
The dashboard and all reports show amounts converted into your default currency, using the rate that was locked when each invoice was sent.

## Changing your default currency
You can change the default currency in Settings > Company only until you send your first invoice. After that, contact support and we will help you.
