# Support hours and how to contact us

## How to reach us
You can contact the Acme Invoicing support team in two ways: by email at support@acme-invoicing.example, or through in-app chat. To start a chat, click Help in the app and choose "Chat with us".

## Support hours
Our team is available Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. We are closed on public holidays in the United Kingdom.

## How fast we reply
Email replies usually arrive within 1 business day. Customers on the Business plan get priority email support, with replies within 4 business hours.

## Outside support hours
You can email us at any time. Messages that arrive outside support hours are answered on the next business day. In-app chat is offline outside support hours.

## What to include in your message
To help us solve your problem faster, please include:
- the email address of your account,
- the invoice number, if your question is about an invoice,
- any error code you saw, such as ERR-4102,
- a screenshot if it helps.

## Billing questions
Questions about your subscription, charges or refunds go through the same email address and chat.
